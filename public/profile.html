How Pay on Delivery works

The customer pays you cash, so Core Insight can't collect its 10% automatically. Instead you keep the 10% in this wallet.

1 · ACCEPT10% of the order is reserved from your available balance.
2 · DELIVERYou ship, the customer pays you cash and gives you their code.
3 · 7 DAYSThe customer can still ask for a refund during the window.
4 · FEE TAKENAfter the window the reserved 10% goes to Core Insight. If the order is cancelled or refunded, it comes back to you.
